Generated by `go test ./cmd -run ClusterMatrix -update-matrix` from `docs/support-matrix.tsv`; do not edit by hand.

Each cell is one model on one chip, over one fabric, in one serving mode, on one engine, simulated with 4 requests of 256 prompt and 16 output tokens. A model's tensor-parallel size is the smallest of [1 2 4 8 16 24 32 48 64] whose weights fit the chip, and its block size the smallest of [16 64 256] the engine accepts. A refusal is one of two kinds: a limit (the model does not fit the device, or vLLM's own backend for it does not support the mode; requirements section 5.2 allows both) or a defect (BLIS refuses for lack of a modelled feature; the issue that holds it is named).
