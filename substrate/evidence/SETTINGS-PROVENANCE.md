commit `catalog/ledger/sources.yaml` pins for the vLLM CI perf source; `vllm` is `vllm-project/vllm`

The revision a run used is the build's checkout, which no public record names. The rule the records allow is the last commit of `workloads/<file>.yaml` with a committer time at or before the row's `date` (`git log` of the file at 713ed1ab). A commit to the file earlier on the row's UTC day exists for 11 rows, and none of them has other `serve_args` than the pinned recipe. `ledger/vllmperf/export.py` does not apply this rule: it reads one checkout, the pinned one, for every row. Of the 6,286 rows that join a recipe by model, GPU, lengths and concurrency (1,509 join none), 559 have other `serve_args` at their date than at the pin, and 116 have no such recipe at their date (counted by `ledger/vllmperf/recipe_revision.py`). Until `export.py` matches by date, the pinned recipe's `serve_args` is a named substitution for those 675 rows | the log's `server_args` (resolved) | the test definition at the commit (the logs have expired) |

So a row's `mean_ttft` is the median over repetitions of each repetition's mean, `median_ttft` the median of medians, and `p99_ttft` the median of the p99s; none is a pooled statistic. At 713ed1ab 13 of the 43 `vllm_bench` configurations set `repetitions: 3` and 30 run once with no aggregation, and 1,725 of the 7,795 rows are dated before 2026-08-10 06:47:32 UTC, when no configuration was repeated. Note 1. Of the 7,795 rows, 218 have an image tag that is a bare 40-digit commit, which is what
`ledger/vllmperf.ImageCommit` accepts. Another 3,091 name a full commit with a suffix
(`<sha>-x86_64`, 2,015 rows; `nightly-<sha>`, 1,076 rows) and are left out as `no_commit_in_image`.
Rows tagged `<sha>-rocm100` or `<sha>-rocm` (2,580) and `<sha>-v0.33.0` or `<sha>-v0.31.0` (234) also
name a commit. 1,153 rows are tagged `nightly`, and about 500 more carry a release, branch, build or
local tag that names no commit. The requirements' "a few rows are not pinned" understates this.
Accepting the suffixed forms is a change to the row 13 ingest, not made here.
